Frequently Asked Questions
You’re not alone. Here are a few questions from other Oklahoma City car owners about our Professional Ceramic Coating costs, process & benefits.
What are ceramic coatings?
A ceramic coating is the highest chemical grade form of protection and gloss for your vehicle. A true coating chemically bonds to your paint and leaves a hardened and hydrophobic layer to your surface that becomes semi-parmanent. This sacrificial layer is extremely chemical resistant and helps in offering protection from bird droppings, bug splatter, sap, and water spotting. It adds a layer of some scratch resistance and protects your car’s surface from harmful UV rays. It also makes your car much easier to keep clean due to it’s rejection of water and the elements. A ceramic coating’s chemical bond differs from waxes and (ceramic) sealants as they only sit on the paint surface and quickly wear off. We are proudly certified with a global leader in professional-grade coatings.
How much do ceramic coatings cost in Oklahoma City, OK?
The cost of a professionally installed ceramic coating in Oklahoma City ranges from $900 – $1,400 depending on how much polishing is required & the size of the vehicle.
How are ceramic coatings applied?
Strip wash to remove dirt, bird droppings, acid rain, and any previous layers of wax
Iron decontamination to shed any break dust, iron or other metal based contaminates
Clay bar application to remove other embedded contaminates
Wheel and wheel well cleaning
Door jam cleaning
Paint correction service
Panel prepping before the coating is applied
Coating installed
