### BENEFITS OF WINDOW TINTING
OKC Onsite Detailing is the leading provider of top-quality window tinting solutions. Our team is passionate about meeting your needs and ensuring that you have the best possible experience with our products and services.
INCREASED COMFORT
Our auto window films contains a proprietary blend of nano-ceramic particles and IR blocking materials, which results in blocking up to 96% of the infrared heat you feel. This makes the interior of your car much more comfortable.
INCREASED PRIVACY
This advanced film boasts an impressive blend of style and functionality, ensuring your privacy remains intact, without sacrificing clarity. Our auto window films provide sleek and sophisticated appearance, and is offered in a variety of shades.
ENHANCED TINT STYLE
Our auto window films add a touch of elegance to your car’s exterior, enhancing its overall aesthetic appeal. Customize your vehicle with a choice of various tint shades. Whether you prefer light tint shades or dark privacy glass options, we have the right film for you.
UVA/UVB PROTECTION
Discover unparalleled protection against harmful UVA/UVB rays with our auto window films. UVA rays can penetrate windows, leading to premature aging, skin damage, and an increased risk of skin cancer. Engineered with the utmost care for your well-being, this revolutionary film offers an exceptional 99% UVA protection.
ZERO SIGNAL INTERFERENCE
Our advanced nano carbon technology allows for optimal transmission of cellular, GPS, and radio signals, ensuring that you stay connected. With our auto window films, you can enjoy the benefits of privacy, heat rejection, and UV protection without sacrificing your connectivity.
LIFETIME WARRANTY
Our auto window films are guaranteed not to fade or turn purple. No bubbling, no peeling and no cracking for the LIFETIME of the Film. Rest assured, you get long term great looks, outstanding color, heat rejection, UV filtering, and all-around protection.
